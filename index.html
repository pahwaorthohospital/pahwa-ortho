About Us

Dr. Manmohan Pahwa

Dr. Manmohan Pahwa, M.S. (Ortho), is an Orthopaedic & Orthobiologic Specialist with over 30 years of experience. He founded Pahwa Ortho Hospital in Fatehabad, Haryana as a committed centre for trauma, pain, and orthobiologics — combining advanced surgical care with compassionate, patient-first treatment.
